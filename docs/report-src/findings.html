Summary

  • Best answers: Claude Haiku 5.5 (OpenRouter). 12 of 14 questions, 51 of 54 checks, about 10 s per question, $0.021 for the whole check. ✅
  • Best value: GPT-6 Luna (OpenRouter). 11 of 14 questions, 49 of 54 checks, about 11 s per question, $0.007 for the whole check: a third of Haiku's cost. ✅
  • Best local model: qwen3.8:27b. 11 of 14, 50 of 54 checks, free, but about 25 s per question. gpt-oss:20b is the fast local option (10 of 14, 8 s). ✅
  • GPT-5-mini works with tools, but isn't worth it here: 9 of 14, 20 s per question, and the most expensive at $0.071 (its hidden reasoning counts as output). ✅
  • Best embedder: mxbai-embed-large (local, free). Hybrid hit rate at 5 0.82, MRR 0.69, and the widest gap between on-topic and off-topic scores. The paid OpenRouter embedder came third of four. ✅
  • Most failures are shared by nearly every model, so they point at our checks and tools, not at the models (see "What still fails"). ✅
  • Total spend for all runs: about $0.20 of the $5 cap. ❓ Summed from the run files plus an estimate for the index; not checked against the OpenRouter bill.

  • The citation rule only covered search. The system prompt said "cite sources as [n]" in the search_security line. No model cited the sources of prioritise, lookup_cve or check_affected, Haiku included. The rule now names every tool. Haiku now cites them; the local models still don't on "what should I fix first?". ✅
  • Unicode hyphens hid CVE ids. gpt-oss and granite write CVE‑2025‑3248 with U+2011 non-breaking hyphens. It looks right, but it can't be searched or pasted into NVD, and the checks didn't see it. Answers now use the plain hyphen. This alone moved gpt-oss:20b from 4 to 10. ✅
  • Curly apostrophes failed the key-fact check. Answers write "can’t" with a curly apostrophe (U+2019); the check looked for a straight one ('). It now treats them the same. ✅

Embedders and the off-topic threshold

  • Hybrid search (vector plus keyword) beats vector alone for every embedder: hit rate at 5 up by 0.26–0.33. ✅
  • For the threshold, each embedder's best match score was saved for the 39 retrieval questions and for 15 clearly off-topic ones (pasta, football, Rome trips). The "Off-topic threshold" chart shows every score. ✅

  • The thresholds were picked with 8 more hand-written security questions as well, such as "Is it safe to let an agent run shell commands?". Their lowest scores were 0.46 (text-embedding-3-small) and 0.68 (mxbai): still above the thresholds. That's why the OpenRouter threshold sits at 0.35, not halfway up the chart's gap. ✅
  • The new thresholds are the defaults now, per model (commit 4b49071). SEARCH_MIN_SCORE still overrides them. ✅
  • 15 off-topic questions is a small sample. ❓ Real off-topic questions may score higher.
  • Why mxbai-embed-large and not qwen3-embedding: both reach a hit rate of 0.82 and an MRR of 0.69. But mxbai separates on-topic from off-topic by 0.21, qwen3-embedding by only 0.04. ✅
  • The keyword search has no threshold. BM25 found "matches" for 13 of the 15 off-topic questions, through words like "poem" or "guitar" in ATLAS case studies. ✅
    • In the chat this didn't matter: no model searched for the pasta question. ✅
    • It would matter if a model searches anyway, and in the search playground. ❓
    • Proposed fix: use keyword hits only when the vector search finds the question on-topic. Not built yet.

Problems found along the way

  • The app said "Load or pull it first" when OpenRouter refused a model. That is the advice for local models. It now passes on OpenRouter's own reason. ✅
  • Ollama 0.31 couldn't load qwen3.8:27b; 0.40.4 can. The Ollama installer also adds a system service that keeps its models in another folder; it was turned off. ✅
