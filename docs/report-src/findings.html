  • The citation rule only covered search. The system prompt said "cite sources as [n]" in the search_security line. No model cited the sources of prioritise, lookup_cve or check_affected, Haiku included. The rule now names every tool. Haiku now cites them; the local models still don't on "what should I fix first?". ✅

  • Curly apostrophes failed the key-fact check. Answers write "can’t" with a curly apostrophe (U+2019); the check looked for a straight one ('). It now treats them the same. ✅

Their lowest scores were 0.46 (text-embedding-3-small) and 0.68 (mxbai): still above the thresholds. That's why the OpenRouter threshold sits at 0.35, not halfway up the chart's gap. ✅
  • The new thresholds are the defaults now, per model (commit 4b49071). SEARCH_MIN_SCORE still overrides them. ✅
  • 15 off-topic questions is a small sample. ❓ Real off-topic questions may score higher.
  • Why mxbai-embed-large and not qwen3-embedding: both reach a hit rate of 0.82 and an MRR of 0.69. But mxbai separates on-topic from off-topic by 0.21, qwen3-embedding by only 0.04. ✅
  • The keyword search has no threshold. BM25 found "matches" for 13 of the 15 off-topic questions, through words like "poem" or "guitar" in ATLAS case studies. ✅
    • In the chat this didn't matter: no model searched for the pasta question. ✅
    • It would matter if a model searches anyway, and in the search playground. ❓
